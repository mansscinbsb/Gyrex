About GYREX

GYREX brings the roomy silhouettes of streetwear together with the practical details of mountain clothing.

We start with boxy puffers, relaxed cuts and enough room to layer as the weather changes. The details matter in everyday wear. We design around warmth, freedom of movement, water-repellent finishes, reliable zippers and pockets you can reach easily.

Make the jacket yours

We’re planning an online jacket configurator. Start with a design you like, then choose colors for individual panels, a hood style and a fastening. Where the design allows, you’ll be able to choose between gloves and mittens.

The idea is to give you room to build a jacket around your own style and the way you’ll wear it.
